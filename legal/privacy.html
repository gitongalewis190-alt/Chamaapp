Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-19-v4 · Effective 19 November 2026

The short version. We collect only what your chama needs to keep its records. We do not sell your data, show adverts, or use your data to make credit decisions. Members see their own money and the group totals; only the treasurer sees everyone’s records. You can see, correct, download or delete your data, and complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

1. Who we are

My Chama App (“My Chama”, “we”, “us”) is a record-keeping app for chamas: savings groups whose members contribute, lend to each other and share payouts. This policy explains how personal data is handled when you use the app at chama-livid.vercel.app or as an installed web app.

Privacy contact (data protection officer or responsible person): [privacy email address]. Write to this address for any request in this policy.

2. Two roles: us, and your chama

We are the data controller for your account: your sign-in details, profile and how you use the app.

For a chama’s records (members, contributions, loans, fines, payouts and expenses), the chama, acting through its treasurer, decides what is recorded and why. For those records we act on the chama’s behalf, to provide the service the chama asked for. The treasurer is responsible for having a lawful reason to record each member’s information and for telling members that the chama uses My Chama. We help with this: members are shown this policy and must accept it before they sign in.

5. Who can see your data

  • Your treasurer sees and can change all of the chama’s records.
  • Members see their own contributions, loans, fines and payouts, the group totals, the member names and photos, and the chama’s expenses. They never see another member’s money, phone number or email.
  • Secretaries see what members see, plus the member register (names and contact details) so they can run meetings, keep minutes and help approve new people. They do not see other members’ money.
  • Messages. Everyone in the chama sees group messages. A private message is seen only by the person who wrote it and the person it was sent to. Join requests are seen by the treasurer and, for non-treasurer roles, the secretary.
  • Our service providers process data only on our instructions, under contract:
    • Supabase, Inc. (database, sign-in and server functions). Data is stored in the European Union (Ireland).
    • Vercel, Inc. (website hosting). Web requests may be served from data centres outside Kenya.
    • Brevo, formerly Sendinblue (sending invitation and sign-in emails), European Union.
    • Google LLC, only if you choose Continue with Google, to confirm your identity.
  • WhatsApp, only when a treasurer or member taps a Share or Remind button. The app opens WhatsApp with a message the person can read and edit before sending.
  • Authorities, where Kenyan law requires it, for example a court order.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data.

8. How we protect it

  • All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Access rules are enforced on the server for every read and write. Hiding a button is never the only protection.
  • Passwords are held only by our sign-in provider, in hashed form. Password attempts are limited.
  • Invitation links and invite codes are stored only as one-way hashes.
  • Payments carry a request key so a double tap or a retry cannot record the same payment twice.
  • Saving uses version checks, so one person’s changes cannot silently overwrite another’s.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach puts your rights at risk, we will notify the Data Protection Commissioner within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, as section 43 of the Act requires, and tell the people affected without undue delay, with advice on what to do.

9. Your rights

Under sections 26 to 38 of the Data Protection Act, 2019 you have the right to:

  • be informed of how your data is used (this policy);
  • access your data;
  • object to the processing of all or part of your data;
  • have false or misleading data corrected;
  • have data deleted, where it is no longer needed or was processed unlawfully;
  • receive your data in a structured, commonly used format (portability);
  • not be subject to a decision based only on automated processing that significantly affects you;
  • withdraw your consent at any time. This does not affect what was done before.

How to use them. In the app: Settings → Privacy and my data lets you download your data and delete your account; Profile lets you correct your details. For anything else, write to the privacy contact. We will reply without undue delay and within the time the law allows, and we may ask you to confirm your identity first. Requests are free unless they are clearly unfounded or excessive.

Records about you kept by your chama. To correct a contribution, loan or fine, ask your treasurer: they keep those records. If they do not help, contact us.

10. Deleting your data

  1. Leave every chama you belong to (Settings → Chama settings → Leave this Chama). A treasurer must first hand the chama to someone else.
  2. Open Settings → Privacy and my data → Delete my account.
  3. Your account is held for 30 days. Sign in during that time to restore it. After 30 days your sign-in, profile and photo are erased.

If you signed in with Google, you can also remove My Chama’s access from your Google Account settings. To ask for deletion without signing in, write to the privacy contact from the email address on your account.

11. Children

My Chama is for adults. You must be 18 or older to create an account. We do not knowingly collect data about children. If you believe a child has an account, tell the privacy contact and we will delete it. A chama should not record a child as a member without a parent’s or guardian’s consent, as section 33 of the Act requires.

12. Cookies and storage on your device

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The app keeps a few items in your browser’s storage because it cannot work without them: your sign-in session, your language and display choices, whether you accepted this policy, and a copy of your chama’s records for offline use. A service worker stores the app’s own files so it opens quickly and works offline.

13. Complaints

Please contact us first so we can try to put things right. You also have the right to complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner, Kenya.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will show you the new version in the app. Continuing to use the app after that notice means you accept it. We keep a record of which version you accepted, when and how, and you can see that record in the app under Settings → Privacy and my data. Earlier versions are available from the privacy contact.
